# F21 Job Profile Reconciliation — Privacy & Information Governance

**Status:** employment-model reconciliation complete  
**Date:** 20 September 2026

All F21 source Job Profiles now have an explicit employment treatment.

Leadership: Head of Privacy & Information Governance.

Professional roles retained: Privacy Manager; Privacy Operations Specialist; Privacy Risk Specialist; Consent & Preferences Specialist; Data Rights Specialist; Privacy Incident Manager; International Data Transfer Specialist; Information Retention Specialist; Privacy Assurance Specialist.

## Employment-model principle

These remain distinct jobs because processing inventory, DPIA, consent, rights cases, incidents, international transfers, retention and assurance have different statutory clocks, evidence, authority and confidentiality requirements.

## Control findings

Processing Activity and DPIA must be separate but linked. International Transfer needs its own assessment/safeguard decision record. Rights requests require identity verification, search/redaction/exemption and deadline evidence. Privacy incidents require point-in-time notification decisions. Retention must share a single precedence model with Records Management and Legal Hold.
