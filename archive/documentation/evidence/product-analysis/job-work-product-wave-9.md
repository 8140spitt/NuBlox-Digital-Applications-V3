## Critical controls

- Risk, control, compliance, audit, remediation and assurance evidence remain linked but distinct.

- Asset identity is never deleted when disposed; lifecycle history remains immutable.
- Occupational-health data is purpose-limited and separated from ordinary H&S incident access.
- Crisis/security investigations are restricted cases with attributable evidence.
- Crisis Communications Manager carries both F24.07 and F25.12 source provenance.
