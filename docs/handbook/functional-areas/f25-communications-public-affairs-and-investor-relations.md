# F25 — Communications, Public Affairs & Investor Relations

## Why this functional area exists

Manage the organisation's corporate narrative, reputation and strategic relationships with employees, media, government, investors, communities and other stakeholders.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define corporate communications strategy;
- manage internal/leadership communications;
- manage media relations;
- plan PR/reputation campaigns;
- monitor reputation/sentiment;
- manage public affairs and policy monitoring;
- manage government relations;
- prepare investor communications;
- coordinate annual reporting;
- map/manage stakeholders;
- manage community engagement and sponsorships;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Communications Director;
- Corporate Communications Manager;
- Internal Communications Manager;
- Media Relations Manager;
- Public Affairs Director;
- Government Relations Manager;
- Investor Relations Director;
- Stakeholder / Community Engagement Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## What people do day to day

- draft/review communications;
- respond to media enquiries;
- monitor reputation/news;
- coordinate executive messaging;
- engage government/stakeholders;
- prepare investor/results materials;
- manage annual-report inputs;
- track commitments;

## Core information and data

- audiences;
- stakeholders;
- media contacts;
- messages;
- communications calendar;
- issues/reputation signals;
- government/policy topics;
- investors;
- annual-report content;
- community commitments;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- communications strategy;
- internal announcement;
- press release;
- media response;
- PR campaign;
- reputation issue/response;
- government engagement/submission;
- investor pack;
- annual report;
- stakeholder map/plan;
- community commitment;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- message/content approval;
- media response;
- reputation response;
- government/policy position;
- investor disclosure approval;
- annual-report approval;
- sponsorship/community commitment;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- message reach/engagement;
- media sentiment/share;
- reputation index;
- stakeholder engagement;
- investor enquiry/coverage;
- reporting timeliness;
- public-affairs outcomes;
- community commitments;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F01/F02 Leadership/Governance for messaging;
- F04 Corporate Development for deal communications;
- F14 Finance for results;
- F19 Legal for disclosure/review;
- F24 Crisis for emergency messaging;
- F06 Marketing for brand/channel coordination;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- communications calendar and controlled content;
- media/stakeholder registers;
- approval/publication workflow;
- reputation monitoring/issues;
- public-affairs/government engagement records;
- investor/results reporting packs;
- annual-report content workflow;
- community commitment tracking;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
