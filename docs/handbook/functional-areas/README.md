# Functional Areas of a Construction & Built-Environment Business

This is the primary functional model of NuBlox Construction & The Built Environment.

NuBlox is a **single enterprise-grade software system through which every functional area of a sophisticated construction and built-environment business can operate**.

The functions below are not software modules. They are the real areas of business responsibility found across the enterprise. NuBlox provides each area with the processes, data, transactions, controls, decisions, reporting and day-to-day working environment it needs, while all areas share one connected enterprise model.

## Product test

For every functional area, the question is:

> **Can the people responsible for this function carry out the complete function in NuBlox without depending on disconnected departmental software or spreadsheets for its core enterprise work?**

A dashboard, menu entry or data register is not sufficient.

## Governance, Delivery and Deployment

Every functional area is defined through the common [Functional Governance, Delivery & Deployment](../02-functional-governance-delivery-and-deployment.md) model.

That means every F01-F29 area must make explicit:

- **Governance** — ownership, standards, process rules, competence, controls, authority, baselines/change and assurance;
- **Delivery** — processes, activities, participating jobs, deliverables, transactions, decisions and handoffs;
- **Deployment** — the actual organisations, Positions and people performing the capability for a project, contract, site, asset or enterprise context.

The [84 Construction & Built Environment Jobs](../construction-jobs/README.md) are a sector-delivery test across these functions. Jobs participate in multiple functions rather than being artificially allocated one-to-one.

## How to read each functional-area chapter

Every chapter describes:

1. why the function exists;
2. what it is responsible for;
3. typical people who work there;
4. its core business processes;
5. what people do day to day;
6. the data/information it uses;
7. its transactions, records and outputs;
8. its decisions and controls;
9. its performance measures;
10. its interactions with the rest of the business;
11. what NuBlox must provide for the function to be genuinely operable.

## One business, not 29 silos

The functions collaborate around shared business reality.

For example, winning and delivering a construction project can involve:

```text
F06 Marketing
  -> F07 Sales / Commercial
  -> F14 Finance + F19 Legal
  -> F27 Project Management
       -> F09 Procurement
       -> F13 Quality
       -> F23 HSE
       -> F14 Finance
       -> F22 Assets
       -> F26 Information
  -> F12 Field / Service Operations
  -> F22 Asset / Facilities Operations
```

The same Customer, Supplier, Project, Contract, Work Package, Item, Asset, Person and financial consequences are reused throughout.

The functional-area model defines **who in the business needs to do what**. The canonical enterprise model defines **the shared business truth they operate on**.
