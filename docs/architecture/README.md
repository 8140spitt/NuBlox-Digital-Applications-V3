## Governing proposition

NuBlox governs organisational capability, deploys that capability through people and operating contexts, enables work through native domain tools, controls the resulting enterprise objects and deliverables throughout their lifecycle, and preserves the authority, configuration, decisions, evidence and records required to operate and assure the enterprise.
