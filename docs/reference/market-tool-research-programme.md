## Architectural rule

A vendor does not create NuBlox architecture merely because its product exists. Alternative products are retained as market/equivalence evidence. A new or extended canonical NuBlox capability is justified only by a materially different job operation, calculation, business object, work product, lifecycle, authority model or evidence requirement.

## Completion condition

The overall benchmark is complete enough to freeze NuBlox tool architecture only when every material enterprise and CBE capability can answer:

> What does the person do, which market operation enables it, what controlled output results, and what equivalent or improved governed NuBlox capability must exist?
