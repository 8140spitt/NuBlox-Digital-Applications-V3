# F19 — Legal & Corporate Secretariat

## Why this functional area exists

Protect the organisation's legal position, manage corporate/legal obligations and provide controlled legal support to enterprise decisions and transactions.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- provide legal advice;
- draft/review/negotiate contracts;
- maintain contract obligations and milestones;
- manage legal entities and statutory registrations;
- operate company-secretarial records;
- manage intellectual property;
- manage disputes/litigation;
- interpret regulation and legal change;
- provide employment-law advice;
- manage external counsel and legal spend;
- manage legal holds and eDiscovery;
- maintain legal-obligation register;

## Typical people who work in this function

- General Counsel / Legal Director;
- Legal Counsel;
- Commercial Contracts Counsel;
- Company Secretary;
- IP Counsel;
- Litigation Counsel;
- Regulatory Counsel;
- Employment Counsel;
- Legal Operations Specialist;
- Contract Administrator;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## What people do day to day

- review contracts and business questions;
- draft legal advice;
- track obligations/renewals;
- prepare filings/resolutions;
- manage legal matters/counsel;
- coordinate disputes/evidence;
- monitor regulatory changes;
- issue/police legal holds;

## Core information and data

- parties;
- contracts;
- legal entities;
- obligations;
- statutory registers;
- IP rights;
- legal matters;
- proceedings;
- external counsel;
- legal spend;
- holds/custodians;
- laws/regulations;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- legal advice;
- contract/redline;
- executed agreement;
- obligation register;
- entity filing;
- board/corporate resolution;
- IP registration/licence;
- litigation matter/evidence;
- regulatory memorandum;
- legal hold;
- eDiscovery production;
- legal obligation;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- legal approval/position;
- contract legal acceptance;
- settlement;
- external counsel appointment;
- legal hold/release;
- regulatory interpretation/advice;
- litigation strategy;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- contract review cycle time;
- legal spend;
- matter volume/age;
- obligation compliance;
- litigation exposure;
- filing timeliness;
- outside-counsel performance;
- hold/eDiscovery compliance;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F02 Governance for statutory decisions;
- F07/F09 for customer/supplier contracts;
- F14 Finance for legal spend;
- F15 HCM for employment law;
- F20 Risk/Compliance for obligations;
- F21 Privacy for legal holds/data rights;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- privileged legal matter workspace;
- contract drafting/review/obligation tracking;
- entity/statutory registers;
- IP lifecycle;
- litigation/dispute cases;
- regulatory change tracking;
- external counsel/spend management;
- legal hold/eDiscovery;
- restricted evidence and privilege controls;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
