# F11 — Manufacturing / Production Operations

## Why this functional area exists

Plan and execute production safely, efficiently and traceably, converting materials and resources into controlled outputs.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- plan production quantities and capacity;
- schedule work centres, labour and machines;
- stage and issue materials;
- release and execute production/work orders;
- capture process parameters and in-process quality;
- manage WIP and production movements;
- package/label finished goods;
- measure yield, scrap, downtime and throughput;
- coordinate maintenance constraints;
- drive lean/continuous improvement;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Operations / Manufacturing Director;
- Production Manager;
- Production Planner;
- Shift / Production Supervisor;
- Manufacturing Engineer;
- Production Operator;
- Lean / CI Specialist;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Production planning;
- Detailed scheduling;
- Material staging;
- Work-order execution;
- Process control;
- WIP control;
- Packaging;
- Production reporting;
- Capacity management;
- Maintenance coordination;
- Lean improvement;

## What people do day to day

- review production plan/schedule;
- release work orders;
- allocate labour/machines;
- record material consumption/output;
- monitor WIP/process conditions;
- record downtime/scrap;
- coordinate equipment availability;
- close production orders;

## Core information and data

- items/BOMs/specifications;
- production plans;
- work centres;
- machines;
- labour;
- work orders;
- material lots;
- WIP;
- process parameters;
- quality results;
- output/scrap/downtime;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- production plan/schedule;
- work order;
- material issue/consumption;
- operation execution;
- WIP movement;
- process control record;
- pack/label record;
- production result/performance report;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- release/hold production;
- reschedule/reallocate capacity;
- process adjustment;
- accept/reject/quarantine output;
- capacity change;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- schedule attainment;
- throughput;
- OEE/availability;
- yield;
- scrap;
- downtime;
- WIP;
- labour productivity;
- unit cost;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F10 Supply Chain for material planning/inventory;
- F13 Quality for inspections/NCR/CAPA;
- F22 Asset/Maintenance for equipment;
- F14 Finance for cost accounting;
- F23 HSE for safe operations;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- production planning/scheduling;
- work-centre and resource views;
- work-order execution;
- material/lot traceability;
- WIP and movement control;
- process parameter capture;
- production quality/evidence;
- production dashboards and constraint management;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
