# 05 — People, Jobs & Responsibilities

## Purpose

NuBlox connects the organisation's people structure to the work the enterprise actually needs performed.

## Core model

Person -> Worker Relationship -> Position Assignment -> Position -> Job Profile -> Functional Responsibilities -> Business Processes / Activities -> Required Outputs / Deliverables -> Current Work.

## Person and worker relationship

Person is the human identity. Worker Relationship is the effective employment, contractor or comparable engagement with an Organisation.

## Position

A Position is a specific seat in the organisation, for example Senior Quantity Surveyor — Project Alpha, Design Manager — Northern Region, or Accounts Payable Specialist — UK Finance.

## Job Profile

A Job Profile is a reusable professional/employment definition such as Architect, Quantity Surveyor, Estimator, Planner, Buyer, Project Manager, Design Manager, Safety Inspector, Project Accountant, Asset Manager or Maintenance Technician.

## Functional responsibilities

Responsibilities can be enterprise-wide or scoped to a legal entity, organisation unit, project/programme, contract/package, site/property/asset or temporary assignment.

## Required outputs

The job model identifies the types of outputs the role is expected to create, contribute to, review or approve.

This includes transactions, models, drawings, calculations, plans, registers, inspections, decisions, reports, certificates and handover information.

## My Work

My Work answers: What needs my attention now?

It includes assignments, deliverables approaching due dates, reviews, approvals, decisions, exceptions, overdue items, cases, inspections, actions and handoffs.

## Job view

A role-oriented view answers: What am I responsible for because of the Position I occupy?

It can show functional responsibilities, projects/contracts/assets, required deliverables, recurring duties, current workload, performance obligations and authority boundaries.

## Assignment is not authority

Employment job, functional responsibility, work assignment, permission, resource scope, delegated authority, approval authority and segregation-of-duties requirement remain separate.

## External teams

NuBlox must also represent client, consultant, engineer, contractor, subcontractor, supplier, statutory body and operator/maintainer participants.

The same responsibility and deliverable principles apply across organisational boundaries, subject to security and contractual access.
