# F21 — Privacy & Information Governance

## Why this functional area exists

Ensure personal and regulated information is processed lawfully, transparently, securely and for appropriate periods while protecting individual rights.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define privacy governance and policy;
- maintain records of processing activities;
- perform DPIAs/privacy impact assessments;
- manage consent and preferences;
- process data-subject rights requests;
- manage privacy incidents/breaches;
- assess international data transfers;
- govern retention and disposition with legal-hold overrides;
- perform privacy assurance and compliance monitoring;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Data Protection Officer;
- Privacy Director / Manager;
- Privacy Operations Specialist;
- Privacy Risk Specialist;
- Data Rights Specialist;
- Consent / Preferences Specialist;
- Privacy Incident Manager;
- Information Retention Specialist;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Privacy governance;
- ROPA;
- DPIA;
- Consent/preferences;
- Rights requests;
- Privacy incident/breach;
- International transfer;
- Retention/disposition;
- Privacy assurance;

## What people do day to day

- review new/changed processing;
- maintain ROPA;
- run DPIAs;
- handle access/deletion/correction requests;
- verify identities;
- review incidents and notification thresholds;
- assess transfers;
- apply retention/hold rules;
- monitor privacy controls;

## Core information and data

- processing activities;
- personal-data categories;
- purposes/lawful basis;
- data subjects;
- processors/recipients;
- consent/preferences;
- rights cases;
- privacy incidents;
- transfer mechanisms;
- retention schedules/holds;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- privacy policy;
- ROPA;
- DPIA;
- consent evidence;
- rights request/disclosure pack;
- privacy incident/breach assessment;
- transfer assessment;
- retention/disposition record;
- privacy assurance report;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- DPIA approval;
- rights exemptions/redactions;
- breach notification;
- international-transfer approval;
- retention/disposition;
- privacy risk acceptance;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- ROPA/DPIA coverage;
- rights-request timeliness;
- incident volume/severity;
- notification timeliness;
- consent/preference compliance;
- retention compliance;
- privacy audit findings;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F17 Data for datasets/access;
- F18 Security for incident/security controls;
- F19 Legal for legal basis/hold;
- F26 Records for retention/disposition;
- F15 HCM for employee data;
- all functions as processing owners;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- processing activity register;
- DPIA/assessment workflow;
- consent/preference evidence;
- rights-case clocks and disclosure packs;
- privacy incident/breach decisioning;
- transfer assessment;
- shared retention/hold/disposition controls;
- purpose-limited access and audit;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
