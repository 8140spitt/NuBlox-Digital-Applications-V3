# 07 — Process, Workflow, Authority & Evidence

## Business process

A business process describes how the enterprise achieves an outcome across people, functions and business objects.

Examples include lead-to-contract, procure-to-pay, project mobilisation, design review and issue, change control, valuation/payment, incident investigation, recruit-to-onboard and maintain-to-complete.

## Workflow

Workflow coordinates the current work required by a process or lifecycle.

Shared concepts include Workflow Definition, Workflow Instance, Work Item, Assignment, Review Request, Approval Request, Decision Request, Response, Escalation and Handoff.

Workflow coordinates business truth. It does not replace it.

## Lifecycle

A governed business object may have controlled states and transitions.

A transition can require valid current state, mandatory information, evidence, permission, authority, review, approval, segregation of duties, effective date and downstream actions.

## Reviews

Reviews may apply to design deliverables, documents, technical submissions, supplier responses, contracts, estimates, quality records, business cases, policies and reports.

Comments and responses must remain linked to the exact version/revision reviewed.

## Decisions and approvals

A decision should retain subject, requested decision, outcome, decision maker, authority basis, scope/threshold, rationale, conditions, timestamp, evidence and affected business objects.

## Authority

NuBlox separates job responsibility, access permission, data/resource scope, delegated authority, approval authority, financial threshold, contractual authority and segregation of duties.

## Permission denied

A denied action must become a controlled user/business state, not a server crash.

The user should understand the denial and, where appropriate, be able to request access or authority.

## Evidence

Evidence can include files, photos, signatures, attestations, test results, issued representations, correspondence, decision records and external references.

Evidence supports business truth but is not automatically the business object itself.

## Audit

Material actions should preserve actor, tenant, business context, event meaning, authority snapshot, correlation, timestamp and evidence/provenance.

## Handoffs

A process is only end-to-end when the next responsible person or function receives usable business context, not merely a notification that something happened.
