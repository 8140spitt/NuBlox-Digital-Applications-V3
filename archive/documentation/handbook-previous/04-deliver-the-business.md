# 04 — Deliver the Business

## Delivery is cross-functional

Delivering construction and built-environment work is not a separate module or function. It is the coordinated operation of the functional areas around a client, contract, project, programme, site, property or asset.

For example, a project may simultaneously involve F07 Sales & Commercial, F09 Procurement, F13 Quality, F14 Finance, F15 HCM, F19 Legal, F23 HSE, F26 Information Management and F27 Project Management.

The delivery journey below explains how those functions collaborate.

Deliver is the end-to-end value-delivery environment for construction and built-environment work.

The precise route varies by contractor, consultant, developer, owner/operator, manufacturer or service provider, but the underlying operating chain is connected.

## 1. Market, client and opportunity

```text
Market insight
 -> Lead
 -> Account / Customer relationship
 -> Opportunity
 -> Qualification
 -> Pipeline
 -> Pursuit
```

Customer and prospect identity use canonical Party/Organisation records.

## 2. Estimate, tender and proposal

A pursuit may produce:

- estimate identity and controlled versions;
- measurements / take-off;
- cost breakdown;
- rates / build-ups;
- risk and provisions;
- supplier/subcontract quotations;
- tender packages;
- adjudication;
- proposal / quotation;
- approval;
- issue/submission.

An Estimate is not the Contract, and its breakdown is not automatically the Project WBS.

## 3. Contract / appointment

Award or acceptance creates/links the contractual truth:

- parties and roles;
- scope;
- contract/appointment;
- terms;
- obligations;
- commercial packages;
- milestones;
- change mechanisms;
- payment terms;
- insurances/bonds where relevant;
- contract documents.

The Contract and Project remain distinct but linked.

## 4. Portfolio, programme and project initiation

Delivery context may be:

```text
Portfolio
 -> Programme
 -> Project
 -> Stage
 -> WBS
 -> Work Package
```

The project establishes governed context for programme, commercial, information, quality, HSE and execution work without owning every related record.

## 5. Design and information

NuBlox controls information requirements and business state around design and information:

- requirements;
- deliverables;
- information containers;
- revisions;
- review;
- issue/transmittal;
- query/RFI;
- design change;
- approvals;
- status;
- evidence;
- handover information.

Specialist authoring may occur in connected CAD/BIM/engineering applications while NuBlox maintains enterprise provenance and decisions.

## 6. Planning and controls

Project delivery requires:

- WBS;
- schedule;
- baseline;
- progress;
- resource plan;
- cost plan/budget;
- forecast;
- risks/issues/actions/decisions;
- project changes;
- status reports.

Programme, cost and change baselines require explicit version/effectivity and decision evidence.

## 7. Procurement and supply chain

```text
Requirement / package
 -> sourcing
 -> tender / quotation
 -> evaluation
 -> award
 -> supplier/subcontract agreement
 -> requisition / purchase order
 -> expediting / logistics
 -> receipt / delivery
 -> invoice / payment
 -> performance
```

Supplier Party identity remains common throughout.

## 8. Construction and field execution

Delivery execution is structured around:

- site and work areas;
- mobilisation/access;
- work packages;
- method / RAMS information;
- permits and isolations;
- instructions;
- temporary works;
- labour, plant and materials;
- daily records;
- progress;
- inspections/tests;
- quality findings;
- safety incidents;
- evidence capture;
- completion/readiness.

Field/mobile experiences are task-oriented compositions over the same canonical records.

## 9. Commercial and cost control

Commercial delivery may include:

- contract budget/cost structures;
- commitments;
- actuals;
- forecasts;
- valuations;
- payment applications;
- payment certificates/assessments;
- variations/compensation events;
- claims;
- subcontract accounts;
- accruals;
- revenue/cost recognition inputs;
- final account.

Commercial actions are integrated with Finance but do not make Finance the master of the project/contract transaction.

## 10. Change

Change is cross-object:

```text
Driver / issue
 -> proposed change
 -> affected objects
 -> design / scope / cost / schedule / risk impacts
 -> assessments
 -> authority decision
 -> implementation
 -> baseline updates
 -> verification
 -> evidence
```

Project, commercial, design, asset and configuration changes retain their domain semantics while participating in a common change experience.

## 11. Quality, safety and assurance

Quality/HSE activities consume the same delivery context:

- ITPs and quality plans;
- inspections/tests;
- non-conformance;
- defects/snags;
- CAPA;
- permit-to-work;
- hazard/risk assessments;
- incidents;
- environmental controls;
- building-safety/regulatory evidence where relevant.

## 12. Commissioning and handover

Handover is not a file dump.

It coordinates:

- completion status;
- inspection/test results;
- commissioning;
- defects/outstanding work;
- as-built/record information;
- O&M information;
- certificates;
- training;
- asset/system/component identity;
- acceptance;
- handover packages;
- readiness for operations.

## 13. Asset and service continuity

The delivery record flows into operations:

```text
Project / Handover
 -> Asset / System / Component
 -> Maintenance strategy
 -> Maintenance plan
 -> due work
 -> Work Order
 -> assignment / dispatch
 -> execution
 -> inspection/result
 -> failure/follow-up
 -> service history
```

This is a major NuBlox objective: eliminate the break between project delivery and whole-life asset operation.
