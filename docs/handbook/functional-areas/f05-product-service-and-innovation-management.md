# F05 — Product, Service & Innovation Management

## Why this functional area exists

Create, develop, launch, improve and retire the products and services the business sells or uses to deliver value.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- manage product/service portfolio;
- research customer/market needs;
- capture and evaluate ideas/concepts;
- prepare investment/business cases;
- define requirements and designs;
- coordinate prototyping, development and verification;
- plan launch and readiness;
- manage lifecycle enhancements and retirement;
- govern innovation pipeline and experiments;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Product Director;
- Product Manager;
- Service Manager;
- Innovation Manager;
- Product Owner;
- Business Analyst;
- R&D / Development Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Portfolio management;
- Needs discovery;
- Ideation and concept selection;
- Business-case approval;
- Requirements/design;
- Development/prototyping;
- Verification/validation;
- Launch;
- Lifecycle management;
- Retirement;

## What people do day to day

- prioritise portfolio/backlog;
- refine requirements;
- coordinate design/development reviews;
- track tests and readiness;
- analyse adoption/performance;
- raise enhancement/change requests;
- manage retirement impacts;

## Core information and data

- products/services;
- portfolio categories;
- requirements;
- concepts;
- business cases;
- versions/configurations;
- tests;
- launch readiness;
- usage/adoption;
- lifecycle states;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- portfolio register;
- concept assessment;
- investment business case;
- requirements specification;
- design definition;
- prototype/test result;
- launch plan;
- enhancement backlog;
- retirement pack;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- concept selection;
- investment approval;
- design/release approval;
- go-live/launch;
- enhancement priority;
- retire/replace product or service;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- portfolio revenue/value;
- time to market;
- development lead time;
- adoption;
- quality/defect rate;
- innovation conversion;
- lifecycle profitability;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F06 Marketing for market insight/launch;
- F07 Sales for commercialisation;
- F12 Service for service-delivery model;
- F13 Quality for verification;
- F14 Finance for business cases;
- F16/F17 for digital products/data/AI;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- product/service portfolio and lifecycle;
- requirements/backlog management;
- business-case and investment workflow;
- version/configuration control;
- prototype/test evidence;
- launch-readiness coordination;
- enhancement/change management;
- retirement and migration planning;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
