# 12 — Glossary

## Business Object

An authoritative NuBlox representation of a business concept such as Project, Contract, Purchase Order, Asset or Inspection.

## Deliverable Item

A required, planned and governed output associated with a business requirement, job, project, contract, package, asset or other context.

## Managed Output

The broad category covering material things produced by enterprise work, including deliverables, transactions, decisions, operational records and evidence.

## Representation

A file/model/rendered form of a business object or deliverable, such as PDF, DWG, RVT, IFC, XLSX or DOCX.

A Representation is not necessarily the authoritative business identity.

## Information Container

A controlled information identity used for documents, drawings, models, specifications and similar information requiring revision and representation controls.

## Functional Area

One of the F01-F29 areas of business responsibility.

A Functional Area is not automatically a software module or database boundary.

## Job Profile

A reusable professional/employment definition describing expected responsibilities and types of work.

## Position

A specific organisational seat occupied by a Person through an effective Position Assignment.

## My Work

The current actionable queue of assignments, deliverables, reviews, approvals, decisions, exceptions and actions requiring a user's attention.

## Work Item

A workflow coordination unit. It is distinct from a Project Schedule Activity, Work Order or Deliverable Item.

## Work Order

An operational instruction/record for maintenance, service or field execution.

## Workflow

The coordinated routing/state of work around business objects. Workflow does not replace authoritative business state.

## Permission

A security capability to perform an action, normally subject to scope and runtime conditions.

## Delegated Authority

A governed grant allowing a person to exercise defined decision authority for a defined scope, threshold and period.

## Approval Authority

Authority to make a defined approval/decision. It is not implied solely by job title or assignment.

## Canonical Identity

The one authoritative NuBlox identity for a business concept reused across functions and contexts.

## Revision

A controlled change state for information/deliverables where revision semantics apply.

## Version

A controlled version of a business object, plan, model or configuration. Version and Revision are not assumed to mean the same thing.

## Lifecycle

The governed states and transitions of a business object.

## Transmittal / Issue

The controlled act/evidence of issuing defined information or deliverables to defined recipients.

## Acceptance

The governed acknowledgement or decision that an issued item satisfies the applicable requirement, subject to status or conditions.

## Evidence

Information supporting a business occurrence, decision, condition or assertion.

## Record

Authoritative information placed under records-governance and retention treatment without creating a duplicate shadow master.
