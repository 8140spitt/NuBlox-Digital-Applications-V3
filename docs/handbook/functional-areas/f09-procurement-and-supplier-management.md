# F09 — Procurement & Supplier Management

## Why this functional area exists

Ensure the business obtains materials, plant, subcontract services, professional services and other resources at the right quality, cost, risk and time.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define procurement and category strategy;
- analyse spend and supply markets;
- discover/prequalify suppliers;
- run RFx/tender/sourcing events;
- evaluate bids and negotiate;
- create/manage supplier contracts;
- onboard suppliers;
- process requisitions and purchase orders;
- measure supplier performance;
- manage supplier risk and relationships;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Procurement Director;
- Procurement Manager;
- Category Manager;
- Buyer / Senior Buyer;
- Sourcing Manager;
- Supplier Relationship Manager;
- Procurement Analyst;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Spend/category planning;
- Supplier discovery/prequalification;
- Sourcing/tendering;
- Evaluation and negotiation;
- Supplier contracting;
- Supplier onboarding;
- Requisition-to-PO;
- Supplier performance/risk;
- Supplier relationship management;

## What people do day to day

- review demand/requisitions;
- prepare RFQs/tender packs;
- invite suppliers;
- analyse returns;
- negotiate price/terms;
- obtain approvals;
- issue POs/contracts;
- review supplier performance/issues;

## Core information and data

- suppliers/parties;
- categories;
- spend;
- sourcing events;
- requirements/specifications;
- supplier responses;
- evaluation scores;
- contracts;
- requisitions;
- purchase orders;
- supplier KPIs/risks;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- category plan;
- supplier prequalification;
- RFQ/RFP/tender;
- bid comparison/evaluation;
- award decision;
- supplier contract;
- supplier onboarding;
- requisition;
- purchase order;
- supplier scorecard/risk assessment;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- supplier qualification;
- sourcing strategy;
- award;
- commercial approval;
- supplier activation;
- requisition/PO approval;
- supplier suspension/remediation;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- savings/value delivered;
- cycle time;
- contract/PO compliance;
- supplier OTIF/quality;
- supplier risk;
- spend under management;
- maverick spend;
- supplier concentration;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F27 Projects and F22 Assets for demand;
- F07/F19 Commercial/Legal for terms;
- F13 Quality and F23 HSE for supplier assurance;
- F14 Finance for commitments/AP;
- F10 Supply Chain for fulfilment;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- category/spend analytics;
- supplier master/relationship lifecycle;
- prequalification and sourcing events;
- bid evaluation/comparison;
- negotiation/award workflow;
- supplier contracts;
- requisition/PO processing;
- supplier scorecards/risk and corrective actions;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
