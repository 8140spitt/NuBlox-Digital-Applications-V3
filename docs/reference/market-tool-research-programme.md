# Market Tool Research Programme

**Status:** Active  
**Effective:** 21 September 2026

## Purpose

This programme controls the transition from a market-product list to an executable NuBlox capability benchmark.

The governing research unit is:

```text
Job Profile
× Tool
× Product module/workspace
× exact operation/task
× input
× work product
× lifecycle/control
× authority/review
× audit evidence
× interoperability
× NuBlox native requirement
```

## Verification acceptance test

A tool is not considered deeply benchmarked until the applicable product capabilities have evidence for:

1. current product/module identity;
2. exact user operation or workflow;
3. input objects/data;
4. produced business object / Deliverable / transaction / evidence;
5. lifecycle/status semantics;
6. review, approval and Authority semantics where applicable;
7. audit/history/evidence semantics;
8. supported exchange/integration formats where material;
9. affected NuBlox Function / Job Profile mappings; and
10. explicit native NuBlox requirement.

A Job × Tool mapping is not considered complete merely because the product itself has been benchmarked. The exact modules/capabilities applicable to that Job Profile must also be confirmed.

## Research waves

### Completed discovery / active deep verification

- Enterprise-function market discovery seed.
- 84-job market-tool matrix.
- 570 Job × Tool mapping register.
- Wave 1 deep verification: design, structural, planning, estimating, CDE, project management, asset management, GIS and field-service platforms.
- Wave 2 deep verification: common construction execution, review and trade job-management platforms.
- Wave 3 deep verification: analytics/semantic modelling, field/task platforms, document control, GIS, conceptual/civil authoring and regulatory case management.

### Wave 4 — remaining high-reuse tools

Continue using the priority table above, then move into the specialist technical engines and regulatory/commercial products with lower reuse but high job criticality.

### Wave 5 — professional technical engines

Deepen structural, MEP, energy, fire, acoustics, geospatial, transport, survey, fabrication and specialist trade design/calculation products.

### Wave 6 — commercial / contractual / property / regulatory

Deepen CEMAR, Unifier, estimating suites, property/valuation platforms, energy-assessment applications and statutory case systems.

### Wave 7 — full 29-function enterprise benchmark

Join the CBE research back to the 29-Function market register and verify ERP/HCM/CRM/GRC/data/security/legal/finance tools at the same module/task level.

## Completion condition

The research programme is complete enough to freeze NuBlox tool architecture only when every material job capability can answer:

> What does the person do, which current tool operation enables it, what controlled output results, and what equivalent or improved governed NuBlox capability must exist?
