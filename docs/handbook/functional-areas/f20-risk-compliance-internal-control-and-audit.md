# F20 — Risk, Compliance, Internal Control & Audit

## Why this functional area exists

Identify and manage enterprise risk, demonstrate compliance, operate/test controls and provide independent assurance over the business.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define risk framework, appetite and taxonomy;
- identify, assess, treat and monitor risks;
- maintain compliance obligations and attestations;
- define and operate internal controls;
- test control design/operating effectiveness;
- plan and execute internal audits;
- report findings and agreed actions;
- manage remediation across assurance domains;
- assess fraud risk and investigations;
- manage ethics/conflicts/gifts/speak-up cases;
- coordinate assurance coverage and independence;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Chief Risk Officer / Risk Director;
- Risk Manager / Analyst;
- Compliance Manager;
- Internal Controls Manager;
- Internal Audit Director / Manager;
- Internal Auditor;
- Fraud Investigator;
- Ethics / Speak-Up Manager;
- Assurance Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Enterprise risk management;
- Compliance obligations/testing;
- Internal control management;
- Control testing;
- Audit planning;
- Audit execution/reporting;
- Issue/remediation management;
- Fraud risk/investigation;
- Ethics/speak-up;
- Assurance mapping;

## What people do day to day

- update risk assessments;
- monitor KRIs;
- track treatment actions;
- perform compliance/control tests;
- plan/execute audits;
- collect evidence/workpapers;
- raise findings;
- track remediation;
- manage restricted ethics/fraud cases;

## Core information and data

- risks;
- risk appetite/tolerances;
- controls;
- requirements/obligations;
- tests/samples/evidence;
- audit universe;
- audit plans/engagements;
- findings;
- remediation actions;
- fraud/ethics cases;
- assurance providers;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- risk register/assessment;
- risk treatment plan;
- compliance attestation;
- control register/execution evidence;
- control test/finding;
- audit plan/workpaper/report;
- remediation record;
- fraud case;
- conflict/gift register;
- speak-up case;
- assurance map;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- risk acceptance/treatment;
- compliance exception;
- control design/remediation;
- audit rating/finding;
- remediation closure;
- fraud/ethics disposition;
- assurance reliance/coverage;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- risk exposure;
- treatment overdue;
- control pass/failure rate;
- compliance breaches;
- audit plan delivery;
- finding age;
- remediation closure;
- repeat findings;
- assurance coverage gaps;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- all functions own risks/controls;
- F02 Governance/Audit Committee;
- F14 Finance for financial controls;
- F18 Security for cyber controls;
- F19 Legal for legal obligations;
- F21 Privacy for privacy controls;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- risk/control/compliance object model;
- risk assessment/treatment/KRI monitoring;
- control library/execution/testing;
- audit universe/plan/engagement/workpapers;
- finding/remediation workflow;
- restricted fraud/ethics cases;
- assurance mapping;
- independence/conflict safeguards;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
