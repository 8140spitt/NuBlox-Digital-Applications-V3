# 01 — Complete Product Model

## What NuBlox is

NuBlox Construction & The Built Environment is the integrated enterprise system through which a construction and built-environment organisation runs its business, delivers its work and manages the things its people are required to produce.

It is not intended to be a loose collection of modules connected by integration. It is one coherent enterprise environment.

## The four dimensions of the product

### 1. The enterprise itself

NuBlox represents the organisation being operated:

- tenant and enterprise identity;
- legal entities and enterprise groups;
- organisation units, divisions, branches, offices and teams;
- people, workers, positions, jobs and reporting structures;
- customers, suppliers, subcontractors, consultants and partners;
- products, services, materials and items;
- resources, labour, plant, equipment and vehicles;
- properties, sites, spaces, networks and locations;
- portfolios, programmes, projects and jobs;
- contracts, appointments, frameworks and packages;
- systems, assets, components and maintainable items;
- classifications, taxonomies, calendars, currencies and reference data;
- policies, authority structures and enterprise configuration.

### 2. How the organisation operates

NuBlox supports the 29 functional areas required to run the business.

Each function must have enough capability for the responsible department/team to perform its normal work, including planning, transactions, specialist analysis, decisions, approvals, exceptions, reporting, evidence and handoffs.

See the Functional Areas section of the Handbook.

### 3. How the organisation delivers

Construction and built-environment delivery crosses functional boundaries.

The journey may include market/client, opportunity, tender, contract, project mobilisation, design, planning, procurement, construction, commercial control, assurance, commissioning, handover and asset/service operations.

See the Delivery Lifecycle chapter.

### 4. What the organisation produces

People do not merely move through workflows. They create things the business needs.

Examples include drawings, models, specifications, calculations, schedules, programmes, cost plans, estimates, contracts, purchase orders, invoices, inspections, risk assessments, permits, decisions, reports, certificates and handover information.

NuBlox must manage these as governed outputs.

## One system of business truth

The same Organisation must not become separate customer, supplier and subcontractor masters.

The same Project must not be copied into finance, commercial, planning, HSE and document-management silos.

The same Asset should flow from design and commissioning into operation and maintenance.

The same Deliverable must retain its identity as it is authored, reviewed, revised, issued, accepted and handed over.

## Sector breadth

NuBlox must support combinations of:

- main contractors;
- specialist/subcontractors;
- developers and housebuilders;
- architects and designers;
- engineering consultancies;
- project/programme managers;
- cost consultants;
- manufacturers/fabricators;
- suppliers;
- asset owners;
- facilities managers;
- maintenance/service organisations;
- infrastructure/network operators.

## Completeness

NuBlox is complete when real people can perform real business functions, produce real required outputs, pass work across organisational boundaries and reconstruct the complete commercial, technical, contractual, financial and evidential history.
