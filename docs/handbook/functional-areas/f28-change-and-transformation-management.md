# F28 — Change & Transformation Management

## Why this functional area exists

Move the organisation from current state to target state in a controlled way and achieve sustained adoption and measurable benefits.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define transformation target state and roadmap;
- assess business/change impacts;
- identify/map stakeholders;
- plan change communications;
- assess training/skills/readiness;
- design/deliver enablement;
- monitor adoption and resistance;
- plan interventions/reinforcement;
- manage role, organisation, process and technology transitions;
- track transformation decisions/actions and benefits;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Transformation Director;
- Change Director / Manager;
- Organisational Change Manager;
- Change Analyst;
- Change Communications Manager;
- Training / Readiness Lead;
- Adoption Lead;
- Transition Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Transformation planning;
- Change impact assessment;
- Stakeholder management;
- Change communications;
- Training/readiness;
- Adoption monitoring;
- Resistance/intervention;
- Organisation/process/technology transition;
- Benefits realisation support;

## What people do day to day

- update impact/stakeholder assessments;
- prepare communications/training;
- track readiness/adoption;
- manage resistance/actions;
- coordinate role/process/system transition;
- prepare transformation governance packs;

## Core information and data

- target/current state;
- change impacts;
- stakeholders;
- roles/processes/systems;
- readiness;
- training needs;
- communications;
- adoption measures;
- transition plans;
- benefit targets;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- transformation roadmap;
- change impact assessment;
- stakeholder register/plan;
- change communication;
- training/readiness plan;
- adoption dashboard;
- intervention action;
- role/org/process/system transition record;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- target-state/roadmap approval;
- change approach;
- readiness/go-live;
- intervention/escalation;
- transition/cutover;
- adoption/benefit acceptance;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- readiness;
- training completion;
- adoption/utilisation;
- resistance/issues;
- communications reach;
- transition defects;
- benefit realisation;
- change saturation;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F01 Strategy for target outcomes;
- F15 HCM for role/org change;
- F16 IT for technology change;
- F29 Process for process redesign;
- F25 Communications;
- F03 Performance for benefits;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- transformation roadmap;
- impact/stakeholder analysis;
- communications/training plans;
- readiness/adoption dashboards;
- intervention/action management;
- role/org/process/system transition linkage;
- benefit tracking;
- cutover/go-live governance;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
