# F15 — Human Resources / Human Capital

## Why this functional area exists

Plan, acquire, organise, develop, deploy, reward and retain the workforce required to operate and deliver the business.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define people strategy and workforce plans;
- design organisation structures;
- maintain job architecture and positions;
- recruit and select workers;
- perform pre-employment screening;
- onboard workers;
- maintain worker/employment records;
- manage time, attendance and payroll;
- manage compensation and benefits;
- manage performance;
- manage learning/competence;
- manage talent/succession;
- measure engagement;
- manage employee relations and absence;
- offboard workers;
- provide people analytics;

## Typical people who work in this function

- HR / People Director;
- HR Manager / Business Partner;
- Workforce Planner;
- Organisation Design Specialist;
- Talent Acquisition Specialist;
- HR Operations Specialist;
- Payroll Specialist;
- Reward Specialist;
- L&D Manager;
- Employee Relations Specialist;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## What people do day to day

- maintain positions/vacancies;
- progress candidates/offers;
- update worker changes;
- review time/absence;
- run payroll;
- manage performance/learning cycles;
- handle employee cases;
- monitor competence/expiry;
- process leavers and access/equipment handoffs;

## Core information and data

- persons;
- workers;
- organisation units;
- job profiles;
- positions;
- assignments;
- candidates;
- contracts;
- pay/benefit data;
- time/absence;
- objectives/reviews;
- skills/competence;
- training;
- employee cases;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- workforce plan;
- position/job profile;
- recruitment requisition;
- candidate/application;
- screening case;
- offer;
- onboarding checklist;
- worker change;
- timesheet;
- payroll result;
- performance review;
- training/competence record;
- ER/absence case;
- offboarding record;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- position approval;
- candidate selection/offer;
- pay/bonus decisions;
- performance rating;
- training/competence approval;
- employee-relations outcomes;
- termination/offboarding;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- vacancy/time-to-hire;
- turnover;
- absence;
- payroll accuracy;
- training/competence compliance;
- engagement;
- performance distribution;
- workforce cost;
- productivity;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- all functions for workforce demand;
- F14 Finance for payroll/cost;
- F16 IT for equipment/identity provisioning;
- F18 Security for access controls;
- F23 HSE for competence/occupational health;
- F27 Projects for resource deployment;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- organisation/job/position model;
- recruitment and screening;
- onboarding/offboarding orchestration;
- worker master and changes;
- time/attendance/payroll;
- compensation/benefits;
- performance and learning/competence;
- talent/succession;
- employee case management;
- workforce analytics;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
