# F22 — Property, Facilities & Physical Assets

## Why this functional area exists

Plan, acquire, create, operate, maintain, optimise and dispose of the physical property and asset base needed by the enterprise and its clients.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define asset/property strategy;
- plan capital investment;
- acquire assets and property;
- manage construction/asset delivery and handover;
- maintain asset master/hierarchy;
- plan preventive maintenance;
- manage reactive maintenance;
- monitor condition/reliability and predictive maintenance;
- manage facilities services;
- manage space/occupancy;
- administer leases;
- manage utilities;
- dispose/retire assets;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Property Director / Manager;
- Asset Director / Manager;
- Facilities Manager;
- Estates Manager;
- Maintenance Manager;
- Maintenance Planner;
- Reliability Engineer;
- Space / Workplace Manager;
- Lease Administrator;
- Asset Data Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## What people do day to day

- review asset condition/performance;
- plan/schedule maintenance;
- raise/assign work orders;
- manage failures/repairs;
- update asset records;
- manage facilities requests/services;
- review leases/occupancy/utilities;
- plan replacement/disposal;

## Core information and data

- properties/sites;
- spaces/locations;
- assets/systems/components;
- asset classifications;
- maintenance plans;
- work orders;
- condition/readings;
- leases;
- occupancy;
- utilities;
- capital plans;
- asset costs;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- asset strategy;
- capital business case;
- asset/property acquisition;
- commissioning/handover;
- asset master;
- maintenance plan/schedule/work order;
- failure/condition record;
- facilities service record;
- space plan;
- lease record;
- utility report;
- disposal record;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- capital investment;
- acquire/lease;
- commission/accept asset;
- maintenance priority;
- repair/replace;
- space allocation;
- lease renewal/termination;
- asset disposal;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- asset availability;
- maintenance compliance;
- MTBF/MTTR;
- backlog;
- planned vs reactive maintenance;
- facility service SLA;
- space utilisation;
- energy/utilities cost;
- lifecycle cost;
- asset condition;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F27 Projects for capital delivery;
- F09 Procurement for assets/services;
- F12 Field Service for work execution;
- F14 Finance for capitalisation/depreciation/lease accounting;
- F23 HSE/Sustainability;
- F26 Information for asset records;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- property/site/space/asset hierarchy;
- capital planning/acquisition;
- commissioning and handover;
- asset master and lifecycle;
- maintenance plans/work orders/mobile execution;
- condition/reliability;
- facilities/space/lease management;
- utilities;
- disposal with immutable history;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
