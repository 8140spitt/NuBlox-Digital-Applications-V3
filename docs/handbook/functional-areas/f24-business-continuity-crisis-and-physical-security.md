# F24 — Business Continuity, Crisis & Physical Security

## Why this functional area exists

Prepare the enterprise for disruption, coordinate effective crisis/emergency response and protect people, sites and operations from physical threats.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define continuity governance and policy;
- perform business impact analysis;
- define recovery strategies/objectives/plans;
- exercise/test continuity arrangements;
- activate/manage crisis response;
- manage emergency response and people accountability;
- manage crisis communications;
- coordinate disaster recovery with technology/business owners;
- operate physical security/access/patrols;
- manage visitors;
- conduct security investigations;
- manage travel security;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Business Continuity Manager;
- Continuity Analyst / Planner;
- Crisis Manager;
- Emergency Response Coordinator;
- Physical Security Manager;
- Visitor Management Coordinator;
- Security Investigator;
- Travel Security Specialist;
- Disaster Recovery Coordinator;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Continuity governance;
- Business impact analysis;
- Continuity/recovery planning;
- Continuity exercising;
- Crisis management;
- Emergency response;
- Crisis communications;
- DR coordination;
- Physical security;
- Visitor management;
- Security investigation;
- Travel security;

## What people do day to day

- maintain BIAs/plans/call trees;
- monitor threats/disruptions;
- run exercises;
- manage site access/visitors;
- review security alarms/patrols;
- investigate incidents;
- track travellers/advisories;
- coordinate crisis actions/communications when activated;

## Core information and data

- critical processes/services;
- dependencies;
- RTO/RPO/recovery objectives;
- continuity plans;
- crisis events/actions;
- people/sites;
- access/visitors;
- security events;
- investigations;
- travel itineraries/risks;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- BIA;
- continuity plan;
- exercise/test record;
- crisis activation/action log;
- emergency/evacuation/muster record;
- crisis message;
- DR activation/recovery record;
- physical security event;
- visitor pass;
- security investigation;
- travel risk assessment;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- continuity/crisis activation;
- severity/escalation;
- evacuation/emergency response;
- crisis message approval;
- site/visitor access;
- security investigation outcome;
- travel restriction/advice;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- plan/BIA currency;
- exercise completion/findings;
- recovery objective performance;
- crisis action closure;
- security incident rates;
- access/visitor compliance;
- investigation closure;
- travel risk exposure;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- all functions for continuity dependencies;
- F16 IT for technology DR;
- F18 Cyber for cyber crisis;
- F23 HSE for emergency response;
- F25 Communications for crisis messaging;
- F15 HCM for people/accountability;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- BIA/dependency mapping;
- continuity/recovery plans;
- exercise/test/remediation;
- crisis command/action log;
- emergency accountability;
- crisis communications approval;
- physical security/visitor records;
- restricted investigations;
- travel security;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
