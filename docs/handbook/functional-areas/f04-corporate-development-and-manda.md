# F04 — Corporate Development & M&A

## Why this functional area exists

Identify and execute acquisitions, disposals, joint ventures, investments and strategic partnerships that change the shape or growth trajectory of the enterprise.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- maintain corporate-development opportunity pipeline;
- screen targets and strategic opportunities;
- perform valuation and financial modelling;
- coordinate commercial, financial, legal, tax, technical and operational due diligence;
- structure and negotiate transactions;
- prepare approval and completion packs;
- plan integration or separation;
- govern strategic partnerships and alliances;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Corporate Development Director;
- M&A Manager;
- Corporate Development Manager;
- Investment Analyst;
- Integration Manager;
- Transaction Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Opportunity screening;
- Valuation and appraisal;
- Due diligence;
- Transaction structuring and approval;
- Negotiation and completion;
- Post-merger integration;
- Divestiture/carve-out;
- Alliance governance;

## What people do day to day

- research targets/partners;
- update valuations and scenarios;
- coordinate diligence requests/findings;
- maintain issues and conditions precedent;
- prepare investment committee/board papers;
- track integration/separation actions;

## Core information and data

- targets/partners;
- valuations;
- assumptions;
- diligence findings;
- transaction structures;
- terms;
- synergies;
- integration workstreams;
- separation perimeter;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- opportunity register;
- screening assessment;
- valuation model;
- due-diligence report;
- heads of terms;
- transaction approval pack;
- executed agreement set;
- integration/separation plan;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- proceed/stop opportunity;
- valuation range;
- bid/offer authority;
- transaction approval;
- sign/complete transaction;
- integration/separation priority decisions;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- deal pipeline value;
- conversion rate;
- time to diligence/close;
- synergy realisation;
- integration milestone attainment;
- investment return;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F01 Strategy for strategic fit;
- F02 Governance for approvals;
- F14 Finance/Tax for valuation/funding;
- F19 Legal for transaction documents;
- F20 Risk for risk assessment;
- F28 Transformation for integration/change;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- deal/opportunity pipeline;
- valuation/scenario workbench;
- due-diligence data room and findings register;
- transaction approval workflow;
- terms/agreement control;
- integration/separation programme linking;
- synergy/benefit tracking;
- restricted deal-team security;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
