## Employment-model principle

Knowledge, document control, records, retention and search are not one generic information-management job. They remain separately staffable with different governance and evidence responsibilities.

## Control findings

Knowledge strategy is not a Knowledge Article. Captured knowledge must not duplicate authoritative controlled procedures. Controlled documents need revision/effectivity/distribution semantics. Records and retention schedules/holds/disposition are different governed objects. Search must security-trim against source authorization and must not become an independent permission authority. Destruction requires permanent disposition evidence.
