# F02 — Corporate Governance

## Why this functional area exists

Ensure the enterprise is directed and controlled through clear authority, policy, accountable decisions and effective boards and committees.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- maintain governance structures, charters and terms of reference;
- plan and administer boards and committees;
- prepare agendas and controlled meeting packs;
- record minutes, resolutions and actions;
- govern delegated authority and approval thresholds;
- govern corporate policies and policy approval;
- manage ethics declarations and governance exceptions;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Company Secretary;
- Governance Director / Manager;
- Board / Committee Secretary;
- Governance Officer;
- Policy Manager;
- Ethics / Governance Specialist;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Board and committee administration;
- Delegated authority management;
- Policy lifecycle management;
- Executive decision governance;
- Ethics declaration and issue handling;
- Governance effectiveness review;

## What people do day to day

- schedule meetings and compile packs;
- capture decisions, minutes and actions;
- check whether proposed decisions sit within delegated authority;
- route policies for review and approval;
- monitor overdue board/committee actions;
- maintain governance registers;

## Core information and data

- governance bodies;
- membership and appointments;
- meeting calendars;
- agendas;
- delegated authority rules;
- approval thresholds;
- policies;
- resolutions;
- actions;
- ethics declarations;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- board/committee pack;
- minutes;
- resolution;
- decision record;
- action register;
- delegated authority matrix;
- corporate policy;
- ethics declaration/case;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- board and committee resolutions;
- policy approval;
- delegated-authority grants/changes;
- executive approvals;
- ethics/conflict determinations;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- meeting/action timeliness;
- policy currency;
- authority exceptions;
- governance action closure;
- board/committee attendance;
- governance effectiveness findings;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F01 Strategy for strategic approvals;
- F14 Finance for investment/financial authority;
- F19 Legal/Secretariat for statutory governance;
- F20 Risk/Audit for assurance;
- all functions for policies and delegated decisions;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- governance-body and meeting workspaces;
- agenda/pack/minute/resolution lifecycle;
- decision and action registers;
- delegated-authority engine;
- approval-threshold rules;
- policy authoring/version/effectivity;
- ethics/conflict case handling;
- immutable decision evidence and audit history;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
