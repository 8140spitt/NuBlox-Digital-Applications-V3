# 06 — Enterprise Data & Business Objects

## Purpose

All functional areas and delivery processes must work on one coherent enterprise model.

The objective is not one giant table. It is one authoritative meaning for each business concept.

## Foundation identities

Important shared identities include Tenant, Party, Person, Organisation, Legal Entity, Organisation Unit, Worker Relationship, Position, Job Profile, Site, Property, Location/Space, Customer/Supplier relationships, Product/Service/Item, Resource/Plant/Equipment, Portfolio, Programme, Project/Job, Contract/Appointment, Work Package, Information Container, Deliverable Item and Asset/System/Component.

## One identity, many functional views

An Organisation can be customer, supplier, subcontractor, consultant, partner or regulator without becoming several different organisation masters.

A Project can be viewed by project management, commercial, planning, design, procurement, finance, HSE, quality and information management without being duplicated.

## Stable identity and changing state

Where relevant, NuBlox distinguishes stable identity, version, revision, iteration, lifecycle state, effectivity, configuration, representation, working draft and issued/published state.

## Structures

Different structures remain distinct:

- enterprise organisation structure;
- project/WBS;
- cost breakdown;
- contract/package structure;
- product/BOM;
- asset/system hierarchy;
- location/spatial hierarchy;
- process hierarchy.

Mappings between structures are explicit.

## Items, products and assets

NuBlox distinguishes an Item/product definition, supplier/manufacturer relationship, lot/batch/serial identity, stock/inventory state and the physical installed Asset created or assembled from items.

## Business transactions

Transactions are first-class business truth.

Examples include quote, sales order, requisition, purchase order, goods receipt, invoice, payment, journal, stock movement, work order and valuation.

A PDF representation does not replace the structured transaction.

## Controlled information

Controlled information can include drawing, model, specification, report, procedure, policy, certificate, correspondence and technical submission.

It retains identity, revision, representations, metadata, issue state and history.

## Search, reports and analytics

Search indexes, dashboards and analytics can denormalise information for speed and usability. They remain projections of authoritative business truth.

## Integration

External identifiers and source references allow NuBlox to interoperate with specialist applications without adopting vendor schemas as the NuBlox business model.
