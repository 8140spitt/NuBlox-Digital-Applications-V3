# F12 — Service Delivery & Field Operations

## Why this functional area exists

Plan, dispatch, execute and evidence professional, technical and field services at customer, project, property or asset locations.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define service delivery models and service standards;
- plan service capacity and resources;
- schedule appointments/jobs;
- dispatch competent resources;
- execute service/work orders;
- capture field inspections, diagnostics, repair, installation and testing;
- manage professional-services engagements/deliverables;
- obtain customer acceptance;
- monitor SLA/service quality;
- optimise workforce utilisation and cost-to-serve;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Service Director / Manager;
- Field Service Manager;
- Dispatcher / Scheduler;
- Field Engineer;
- Service Technician;
- Professional Services Manager;
- Service Delivery Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Service planning;
- Scheduling;
- Dispatch;
- Work-order execution;
- Field inspection/repair/install;
- Professional services delivery;
- Completion/acceptance;
- SLA quality;
- Capacity planning;
- Service analytics;

## What people do day to day

- schedule and reassign jobs;
- dispatch technicians/engineers;
- review travel/routes;
- capture site attendance/work/evidence;
- record readings/tests/parts/time;
- obtain sign-off;
- close work and trigger billing;
- manage SLA exceptions;

## Core information and data

- customers/sites/assets;
- service entitlements;
- appointments;
- resources/competence;
- work orders;
- routes;
- parts/materials;
- time;
- readings;
- test results;
- customer acceptance;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- service plan;
- appointment;
- dispatch;
- service/work order;
- field inspection/diagnostic;
- repair/install record;
- test/commissioning record;
- customer sign-off;
- service completion;
- billing handoff;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- resource assignment;
- priority/escalation;
- service acceptance;
- rework/follow-up;
- SLA remediation;
- billing readiness;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- response/resolution time;
- first-time fix;
- SLA attainment;
- utilisation;
- travel time;
- jobs per day;
- customer acceptance;
- cost to serve;
- rework;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F08 Customer Service for cases;
- F22 Assets for asset history/maintenance;
- F09/F10 for parts/supply;
- F13 Quality for service defects;
- F14 Finance for billing;
- F15 HCM for competence/resource availability;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- service calendar and dispatch board;
- mobile field work;
- asset/site context;
- checklists/readings/photos/signatures;
- parts/time capture;
- inspection/test and commissioning;
- customer acceptance;
- billing trigger;
- SLA/service-performance dashboards;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
