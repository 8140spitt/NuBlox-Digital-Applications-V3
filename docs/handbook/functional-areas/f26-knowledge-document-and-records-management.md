# F26 — Knowledge, Document & Records Management

## Why this functional area exists

Ensure organisational knowledge and controlled information can be created, found, trusted, reused, retained and disposed of correctly.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define knowledge/information governance;
- capture lessons, experience and know-how;
- publish/share knowledge;
- review/curate knowledge content;
- control documents and information containers;
- declare/manage records;
- manage controlled procedures/documents;
- apply retention schedules and holds;
- provide enterprise search/discovery;
- capture and disseminate lessons learned;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Knowledge Manager;
- Knowledge Capture Specialist;
- Knowledge Curator;
- Document Controller;
- Records Manager;
- Controlled Documents Coordinator;
- Records Retention Specialist;
- Enterprise Search Specialist;
- Lessons Learned Coordinator;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## What people do day to day

- register/revise/issue documents;
- review content;
- declare records;
- apply classifications/metadata;
- manage retention/holds;
- support search/discovery;
- capture lessons and knowledge;
- withdraw/supersede controlled information;

## Core information and data

- information containers;
- files/representations;
- metadata/classification;
- revisions;
- records;
- retention rules;
- holds;
- disposition decisions;
- knowledge articles;
- lessons;
- search index/source metadata;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- controlled document;
- revision/issue;
- knowledge article;
- declared record;
- retention schedule;
- hold/exception;
- disposition certificate;
- lesson learned;
- search/index configuration/evidence;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- document/content approval;
- record declaration/classification;
- hold/release;
- disposition/destruction;
- knowledge publication/retirement;
- controlled document supersession;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- document turnaround;
- metadata completeness;
- search success;
- knowledge reuse;
- record-retention compliance;
- disposition backlog;
- overdue reviews;
- lesson adoption;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- all functions create/use information;
- F19 Legal for legal hold;
- F21 Privacy for retention/data rights;
- F18 Security for access;
- F27 Projects for project information;
- F29 Process for SOPs/lessons;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- information-container/revision/representation model;
- document control/transmittal;
- records overlay without shadow copies;
- retention/hold/disposition;
- knowledge authoring/curation;
- lesson-learned workflow;
- security-trimmed enterprise search;
- immutable disposition evidence;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
