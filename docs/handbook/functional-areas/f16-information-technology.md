# F16 — Information Technology

## Why this functional area exists

Provide reliable, secure and supportable technology services, platforms, applications and devices that enable the enterprise to operate and deliver its work.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define technology strategy, standards and roadmaps;
- manage enterprise and solution architecture;
- manage application lifecycle;
- develop and maintain software;
- operate DevOps/CI-CD environments;
- manage infrastructure, cloud, networks and endpoints;
- provision identities/accounts following approved access decisions;
- operate IT service desk and request fulfilment;
- manage incidents, problems, changes and releases;
- maintain configuration and IT asset records;
- manage availability/capacity and disaster recovery;
- manage technology suppliers and licences;

## Typical people who work in this function

- CIO / IT Director;
- Enterprise / Solution Architect;
- Application Manager;
- Software Engineer;
- DevOps Engineer;
- Infrastructure / Cloud / Network Engineer;
- IT Service Desk Analyst;
- IT Service Manager;
- IT Asset Manager;
- Technology Vendor Manager;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## What people do day to day

- monitor services and infrastructure;
- resolve requests/incidents;
- deploy approved changes/releases;
- provision/deprovision accounts and devices;
- maintain applications/configuration items;
- patch/maintain platforms;
- review capacity and availability;
- manage licences/vendors;

## Core information and data

- applications;
- technology services;
- environments;
- infrastructure;
- networks;
- endpoints;
- configuration items;
- users/accounts;
- incidents/problems/changes/releases;
- IT assets;
- licences;
- vendors;
- capacity/availability metrics;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- technology strategy;
- architecture model;
- solution architecture;
- application baseline;
- software change/release;
- deployment record;
- service request;
- incident/problem;
- change request;
- CMDB/configuration record;
- IT asset record;
- DR plan/test;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- technology standards;
- solution architecture approval;
- application acquisition/build;
- change/release approval;
- service priority/escalation;
- asset/licence decisions;
- recovery invocation;
- vendor renewal;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- availability;
- incident volume/MTTR;
- change success rate;
- service request SLA;
- deployment frequency;
- capacity utilisation;
- asset/licence compliance;
- DR test success;
- technology cost;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- all functions consume technology services;
- F17 Data for data platforms;
- F18 Cybersecurity for security controls/incidents;
- F15 HCM for joiner/mover/leaver;
- F09 Procurement for technology sourcing;
- F14 Finance for IT cost/assets;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- technology/service/application registry;
- architecture and standards repository;
- ITSM request/incident/problem/change/release;
- CMDB/configuration relationships;
- asset/licence management;
- identity-provisioning handoff from approved access grants;
- capacity/availability monitoring;
- DR planning/testing;
- technology vendor and contract linkage;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
