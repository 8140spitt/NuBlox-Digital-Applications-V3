# Sustainability, Carbon, Energy, Circularity & Social Value Semantics

## Purpose

BOF-20 defines sustainability measurement, evidence and target semantics while preserving canonical operational, commercial, physical and financial truth.

The governing flow is:

Carbon methodology / factors -> baseline / budget / target -> authoritative activity and quantity evidence -> assessment / measure -> published outcome.

The central rule is:

> Sustainability interprets, measures and evidences canonical business truth. It does not manufacture duplicate Projects, Items, Assets, Suppliers, utility records or finance data.

## Carbon methodology and factors

Carbon Methodology Definition is versioned controlled calculation policy.

Carbon Factor Source identifies the governed provider or dataset. Individual Carbon Factor Entries are versioned and effective values under that source.

Published assessments pin the exact methodology version, factor source and dataset, factor entry, quantity/activity unit, geography/time context and source business records.

Factor updates never silently rewrite historic published calculations.

## Baseline, budget, target and assessment

These remain different concepts:

- Carbon Baseline Snapshot — historic/reference position.
- Carbon Budget — planned allowance or trajectory.
- Carbon Target — desired future outcome.
- Carbon Assessment — calculated measured or modelled position.

An approved baseline is immutable. A carbon budget does not become a finance Budget. A target does not become actual performance.

## Embodied carbon

The source candidate Embodied Carbon Item is normalized to Carbon Assessment Line.

A line can reference canonical Item/material, quantity and Unit of Measure, Project/WBS/package/activity context, lifecycle module, EPD, carbon factor and provenance evidence.

It never creates another product or material master.

## Operational energy and carbon

Utility Consumption already exists as whole-life operations evidence. BOF-20 reuses it.

Meter / Utility Account / source evidence -> Utility Consumption -> Operational Energy Assessment -> Carbon Factor + Methodology -> Operational Carbon Assessment.

Operational Energy and Carbon records are derived assessments or snapshots, not independently authored consumption truth.

## Waste, recovery, reuse and circularity

Waste Stream is a governed material/waste classification or flow definition.

Actual regulated transfer remains Waste Consignment in the QHSE model.

Recovery Record and Reuse Record are immutable evidence. Circularity Assessment derives metrics from authoritative material-flow evidence.

No circularity calculation rewrites inventory, Item, Asset or QHSE records.

## Environmental Product Declaration

EPD is a governed external declaration with product/Item reference, manufacturer/issuer, programme operator, product category rules, declared/functional unit, lifecycle modules/results, verification, publication/version and validity.

EPD references canonical Item and Organisation identities. It does not replace them.

## Material provenance

Material Provenance Record captures source-linked evidence such as origin, producer/supplier, Lot/Batch/Serial, custody, transformation, certification, recycled/reused content and source documentation.

It references existing Item and inventory identities rather than creating a sustainability material master.

## Responsible procurement

Responsible Procurement Assessment evaluates Party/supplier, sourcing and Item/material evidence against explicit criteria.

It is distinct from Supplier Party identity, Party Relationship, Sourcing Evaluation, Award, Contract and Purchase Order.

## Biodiversity and environmental measures

Biodiversity Measure and Environmental Measure are projections over a defined spatial or business scope.

Every published measure retains canonical Site/Project/Asset scope, spatial boundary where relevant, method/version, period/date, source evidence and quality.

They remain separate from QHSE Environmental Aspect and Environmental Impact.

## Social value

NuBlox separates three layers:

Social Value Commitment -> Social Value Evidence -> Social Value Outcome.

Commitment is the obligation or target. Evidence is what actually happened. Outcome is the measured or assessed result under a stated methodology.

A monetised social-value outcome never becomes Finance ledger truth.

## Climate and resilience risk

Climate Risk and Resilience Risk are typed Enterprise Risks, not separate sustainability risk registers.

They reuse Enterprise Risk, Risk Assessment, Treatment Plan and monitoring/evidence patterns.

Sustainability adds climate/resilience classification, scenario, horizon and affected Site/Asset/service context.

## Cost-carbon option assessment

Cost-Carbon Option Assessment is decision support. It references exact cost/estimate/finance source positions, carbon assessment versions, methodology, option scope, time horizon and assumptions.

It does not become cost truth or carbon truth.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Sustainability never duplicates canonical Item, Asset, Project, Party, Site, Utility or financial identities.
2. Methodologies and factor datasets are versioned and historic calculations pin exact versions.
3. Baseline, Budget, Target and Assessment remain separate.
4. Embodied-carbon lines reference Item/material identity rather than replace it.
5. Operational energy/carbon derives from authoritative utility/activity evidence.
6. Utility Consumption is shared with operations.
7. Waste Stream, Waste Consignment, Recovery, Reuse and Circularity Assessment are distinct layers.
8. EPD references Item/Organisation and retains declaration/version provenance.
9. Material provenance is evidence around canonical material/product/inventory identities.
10. Responsible Procurement Assessment is separate from sourcing decision truth.
11. Biodiversity/environmental measures retain exact scope, method and evidence.
12. Social-value Commitment, Evidence and Outcome are distinct.
13. Climate and Resilience Risk reuse Enterprise Risk.
14. Cost-carbon comparison never overwrites either cost or carbon source truth.
