# F10 — Demand, Supply Chain & Logistics

## Why this functional area exists

Convert demand into feasible supply and move materials, goods and equipment through inventory, warehousing, transport and delivery.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- forecast demand;
- run S&OP/demand-supply balancing;
- create supply/material/replenishment plans;
- set inventory policies and targets;
- operate warehouse receiving/storage/picking/dispatch;
- control stock counts/quarantine/adjustments;
- plan transport and carriers;
- manage distribution/delivery;
- manage import/export/customs;
- manage reverse logistics;
- monitor supply-chain risk/performance;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Supply Chain Director/Manager;
- Demand Planner;
- Supply Planner;
- Materials Planner;
- Warehouse Manager;
- Inventory Controller;
- Logistics Manager;
- Transport Planner;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Demand planning;
- S&OP;
- Supply planning;
- Inventory planning;
- MRP/replenishment;
- Warehouse operations;
- Inventory control;
- Transport;
- Distribution;
- Trade compliance;
- Reverse logistics;

## What people do day to day

- review demand/supply exceptions;
- release replenishment requirements;
- monitor stock/shortages;
- receive/move/pick/dispatch materials;
- track shipments;
- resolve delivery/customs issues;
- manage returns;

## Core information and data

- demand signals;
- forecasts;
- inventory;
- locations;
- materials/items;
- replenishment parameters;
- shipments;
- carriers;
- routes;
- trade classifications;
- delivery commitments;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- demand forecast;
- S&OP plan;
- supply plan;
- inventory target;
- MRP/replenishment schedule;
- goods receipt;
- stock movement/count;
- shipment/load;
- delivery/POD;
- customs pack;
- return/disposition;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- forecast approval;
- inventory policy;
- expedite/defer supply;
- carrier selection;
- stock adjustment/quarantine;
- alternative sourcing/contingency;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- forecast accuracy;
- service level/availability;
- inventory turns;
- stock accuracy;
- lead time;
- OTIF;
- warehouse productivity;
- freight cost;
- supply disruption exposure;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F09 Procurement for supplier commitments;
- F11 Production for material demand/output;
- F27 Projects for site/package demand;
- F14 Finance for inventory/landed cost;
- F13 Quality for receipt inspection/quarantine;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- demand/S&OP/supply planning workbenches;
- inventory and location ledger;
- warehouse mobile transactions;
- shipment/transport planning and tracking;
- trade/customs records;
- POD/evidence;
- reverse logistics;
- supply-chain risk and KPI dashboards;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
