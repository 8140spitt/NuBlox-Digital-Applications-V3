# F17 — Data, Analytics & AI

## Why this functional area exists

Make enterprise data trusted, governed and usable, and convert it into analytics, models and AI capabilities that support better decisions and operations.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define data and AI strategy;
- govern data ownership, stewardship, standards and policies;
- design data architecture and lineage;
- manage master and reference data quality;
- build and operate data pipelines/platforms;
- design BI reports and dashboards;
- perform analytical studies and data science;
- develop and validate machine-learning/AI models;
- govern AI use cases, risk and approvals;
- operate model deployment/monitoring/MLOps;
- maintain metadata/catalogue/business glossary;
- manage approved data access;
- manage dataset/data-product lifecycle and disposition;

## Decisions and controls

- data ownership/stewardship;

- data-quality remediation;
- report/model publication;
- AI use approval;
- model deployment/retirement;
- data access;
- data disposition;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- data quality;
- pipeline reliability;
- report adoption;
- analytics lead time;
- model performance/drift;
- AI risk/compliance;
- data access cycle time;
- metadata/lineage coverage;
- platform cost/performance;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- all functions are data producers/consumers;
- F16 IT for platforms;
- F18 Cybersecurity for access/security;
- F21 Privacy for personal data;
- F26 Records for retention;
- F03 Performance for enterprise measures;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- data catalogue/business glossary;
- master/reference data governance;
- quality rules/issues;
- lineage and architecture maps;
- pipeline/run evidence;
- BI/report lifecycle;
- analysis/model workbench;
- AI use-case/model/version/evaluation governance;
- common access-request/grant integration;
- data retention/disposition evidence;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
