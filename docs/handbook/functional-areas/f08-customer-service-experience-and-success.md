# F08 — Customer Service, Experience & Success

## Why this functional area exists

Onboard, support, retain and grow customer value throughout the relationship while resolving enquiries, complaints and service issues in a controlled way.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- manage customer onboarding and service activation;
- handle enquiries and customer cases;
- manage complaints and remediation;
- provide technical/customer support;
- manage returns/refunds and warranty;
- monitor customer health and success;
- manage churn/retention;
- capture feedback/NPS/CSAT;
- maintain customer-facing knowledge;
- monitor service-level performance;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Customer Service Director/Manager;
- Customer Success Manager;
- Support Manager;
- Customer Service Adviser;
- Technical Support Specialist;
- Complaints Manager;
- Retention Specialist;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Customer onboarding;
- Enquiry/case management;
- Complaint management;
- Technical support;
- Returns/refunds;
- Warranty;
- Customer success;
- Retention;
- Feedback;
- SLA monitoring;

## What people do day to day

- respond to enquiries/cases;
- diagnose issues;
- coordinate resolution/escalation;
- approve/route returns or remediation;
- review customer health;
- plan success/retention interventions;
- analyse feedback;

## Core information and data

- customer/accounts;
- contacts;
- service entitlements;
- cases;
- complaints;
- support logs;
- warranties;
- returns;
- customer health;
- feedback;
- SLA commitments;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- onboarding record;
- customer enquiry/case;
- complaint;
- technical diagnostic;
- return authorisation;
- warranty claim;
- success review;
- retention offer;
- feedback/NPS record;
- SLA report;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- case resolution;
- compensation/remediation;
- return/refund approval;
- warranty eligibility;
- retention offer;
- escalation;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- first response/resolution time;
- first-contact resolution;
- complaint recurrence;
- CSAT/NPS;
- churn/retention;
- customer health;
- SLA attainment;
- cost to serve;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F07 Sales for account/commercial context;
- F05 Product/Service for defects/enhancements;
- F12 Field Service for dispatched work;
- F13 Quality for recurring defects;
- F14 Finance for credits/refunds;
- F25 Communications for sensitive customer issues;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- omnichannel customer case workspace;
- entitlement/SLA visibility;
- support diagnostics and knowledge;
- complaint/return/warranty workflows;
- customer health/success plans;
- feedback and sentiment;
- retention actions;
- cross-functional escalation with full history;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
