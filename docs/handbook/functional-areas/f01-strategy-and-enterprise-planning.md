# F01 — Strategy & Enterprise Planning

## Why this functional area exists

Set the organisation's long-term direction and turn that direction into coherent, funded and measurable enterprise plans.

NuBlox must allow the people responsible for this function to carry out the complete function without relying on disconnected departmental spreadsheets or applications for its core enterprise work.

## What the function is responsible for

- define purpose, vision, mission and strategic principles;
- analyse external markets, competitors, regulation, technology and sector trends;
- define enterprise objectives, priorities and strategic initiatives;
- prepare annual and multi-year business plans;
- design and review the operating model and enterprise capabilities;
- define strategic measures, targets and assumptions;
- run strategic reviews, scenarios and contingency planning;

## Typical people who work in this function

- Chief Executive / Managing Director;
- Strategy Director / Head of Strategy;
- Strategy Manager;
- Enterprise Planning Manager;
- Business Planning Manager;
- Strategy Analyst;

Job titles vary by organisation. The functional area describes the business responsibility; Job Profiles and Positions describe who performs it.

## Core business processes

- Strategy formulation and approval;
- External/environmental analysis;
- Annual business planning;
- Operating-model design;
- Strategic initiative planning;
- Scenario planning;
- Strategic review and refresh;

## What people do day to day

- maintain strategic assumptions and evidence;
- develop option papers and business plans;
- coordinate planning submissions from business units;
- track strategic initiatives and enterprise KPIs;
- prepare executive/board strategy packs;
- reforecast scenarios when market conditions change;

## Core information and data

- enterprise objectives;
- strategic assumptions;
- market/competitor intelligence;
- business-unit plans;
- strategic initiatives;
- enterprise capability model;
- targets and KPIs;
- planning periods and scenarios;

The function consumes shared enterprise data and contributes new governed business state. It must not create private copies of canonical customers, suppliers, projects, people, contracts, assets or other shared objects.

## Key transactions, records and outputs

- enterprise strategy;
- annual business plan;
- business-unit plan;
- strategic initiative register;
- operating-model definition;
- scenario model;
- strategy review pack;

These may be structured records, calculations, controlled information, transactions, decisions, inspections, reports or handover packages. NuBlox must support the appropriate form rather than forcing every output into a document.

## Decisions and controls

- approve strategy and priorities;
- approve annual/business-unit plans;
- approve strategic initiatives;
- change strategic priorities;
- approve operating-model changes;

Authority must be enforced independently from job title. Assignment to work does not itself grant approval, financial, contractual or access authority.

## Performance measures

Typical measures include:

- strategic objective attainment;
- initiative delivery;
- plan-versus-actual performance;
- benefit realisation;
- forecast accuracy;
- strategic risk exposure;

Measures are configurable by tenant and should drill back to the underlying governed data.

## How this function interacts with the rest of the business

- F02 Governance for board/executive approval;
- F03 Performance for KPI/variance feedback;
- F04 Corporate Development for investment/M&A options;
- F14 Finance for affordability and funding;
- F15 HCM for workforce implications;
- F27 PPM for initiative delivery;
- F28 Transformation for target-state change;

The handoffs use shared canonical objects and governed workflows rather than point-to-point copies between departmental systems.

## What NuBlox must provide

For this functional area to be genuinely operable inside NuBlox, the product must provide:

- strategy and objective hierarchy;
- assumption/evidence register;
- business-plan authoring and consolidation;
- initiative portfolio and ownership;
- scenario/calculation workbench;
- operating-model/capability structures;
- strategy review and approval workflow;
- direct linkage from strategy to budgets, programmes, risks, benefits and KPIs;

## Product-completeness test

This function is not complete because a menu item or dashboard exists.

It is complete when authorised people in this functional area can execute their normal end-to-end responsibilities in NuBlox, including the exceptions, approvals, evidence, handoffs and reporting required by a sophisticated construction and built-environment business.
