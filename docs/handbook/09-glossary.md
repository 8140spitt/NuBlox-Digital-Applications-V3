# 09 — NuBlox Glossary

## Access Role

A security grouping used to grant Permissions. It is not an employment Job Profile.

## Activity

A unit of business work performed as part of a Function/Sub-function.

## Aggregate

A consistency/command boundary that owns business invariants and authorised state changes.

## Approval Authority

The authority to approve a defined decision within a scope/threshold. It is not implied by job title.

## Canonical Business Object

The authoritative NuBlox identity and semantic definition for a business concept.

## Controlled Information

Governed information with identity, revision/version, lifecycle, issue/effectivity and evidence controls.

## Delegated Authority

A governed grant allowing a person to exercise defined decision authority for a defined scope/time. It is not the same as work delegation.

## Enterprise Data

Shared operational master/reference data used across Operate and Deliver.

## Function Workspace

One of F01-F29: the stable user-facing home/perspective for an enterprise function. It is not a separate application or database.

## Functional Role

A reusable bundle of functional responsibility/activity provenance. It is distinct from Job Profile and security role.

## Job Profile

A reusable employment-job definition describing expected professional work and Work Products.

## Job Workbench

A runtime composition showing work expected for the current Position/Job Profile together with context, actual permissions/authority and current work.

## My Work

The runtime view of assigned/eligible/pending work requiring the user's attention.

## Party

The canonical identity root for a Person or Organisation.

## Permission

A security capability to perform a protected action, subject to scope and runtime rules.

## Position

A tenant-specific organisational seat to which a Person may be assigned.

## Position Assignment

The effective-dated occupancy relationship between Person and Position.

## Record

An evidence/governance treatment applied to authoritative information. It is not automatically a copied file.

## Task Bar

The user's active personal working contexts/drafts. It is distinct from My Work.

## Work Item

A workflow-coordination unit. It is not a Project Schedule Activity or Work Order.

## Work Order

An operational instruction/record for field, service or maintenance execution against relevant scope/assets/locations.

## Work Product

A material professional output required by a job/activity, such as a Cost Plan, Inspection Record, Contract or Board Pack.

## Worker Relationship

The effective employment/contractor/worker relationship between a Person and Organisation.
