**Status:** Specialist CBE market baseline architecturally closed; enterprise-function deep benchmark remains active

## Purpose

This programme controls the transition from a market-product list to an executable NuBlox capability benchmark.

The governing research unit is:

```text
Job Profile
× Tool
× Product module/workspace
× exact operation/task
× input
× work product
× lifecycle/control
× authority/review
× audit evidence
× interoperability
× NuBlox native requirement
```

## Architectural rule established by Wave 8

A product does not create a NuBlox capability merely because it exists in the market.

Every product is treated as one of:

- **Deep-verified anchor** — representative product used to expose operative semantics.
- **ALTERNATIVE_PRODUCT_PATTERN** — equivalent implementation of an already-understood capability.
- **INTEGRATION_FORMAT_REFERENCE** — important chiefly for data exchange or migration.
- **LOW_RELEVANCE_OR_DUPLICATE** — retained as evidence but does not create new architecture.

Only materially different job operations, controlled objects, calculations, work products, lifecycle semantics or evidence requirements can add or extend the canonical NuBlox capability model.
