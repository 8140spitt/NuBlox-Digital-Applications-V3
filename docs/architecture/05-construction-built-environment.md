# 05 — NuBlox Construction & Built Environment

Construction & Built Environment is the first industry solution on the NuBlox platform. It is not the boundary of the NuBlox product.

## Industry configuration

The solution introduces:

- 16 delivery domains;
- 84 job profiles;
- construction-specific project, contract and package structures;
- sites, facilities, systems, spaces and assets;
- design and technical information;
- construction production and field records;
- inspections, testing and commissioning;
- commercial and contractual deliverables;
- handover and asset-information requirements; and
- industry-specific classifications, lifecycle rules, competencies and evidence.

## Job principle

The 84 job profiles are capability profiles, not 84 applications.

A job profile may participate in multiple enterprise functions and delivery domains with one or more participation modes such as:

- primary;
- delivery;
- governance;
- assurance; and
- support.

Actual work arises through deployment into organisational and delivery contexts.

## Work-product principle

NuBlox must allow each role to create and control the work products required by that role. Examples include drawings, models, specifications, calculations, schedules, estimates, valuations, procurement records, method statements, inspection records, commissioning records, certificates and asset information.

The controlled business identity of those outputs remains separate from any generated PDF, spreadsheet, CAD file or other representation.
